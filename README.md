Product engineer at [Richpanel](https://www.richpanel.com), building the systems behind an AI-powered customer support platform — usage-based billing and metering, AI infrastructure, and the analytics pipelines that report on all of it.

Outside of work I build self-hosted, privacy-first tools and real-time web apps. Most of that lives on my personal account, [@Aaditya188](https://github.com/Aaditya188).

### What I work on

- **Billing and metering** — credit ledgers, entitlement grants, proration, and the Stripe integrations that keep them honest. Mostly the unglamorous correctness work: idempotency, double-spend, reconciliation.
- **AI infrastructure** — usage accounting for LLM features, cost attribution, and the guardrails that sit in front of them.
- **Data and reporting** — moving reporting workloads onto columnar stores, and building the metrics teams actually make decisions on.
- **TypeScript and Python**, NestJS and Node, DynamoDB, ClickHouse, AWS.
